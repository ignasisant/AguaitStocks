When discussing crypto positions (the app tracks pairs like BTC-EUR), keep the frame distinct from equities — there are no cash flows to anchor to:

- **What the asset is**: monetary/store-of-value claim (BTC), smart-contract platform with fee burn (ETH), app token, or memecoin — the investment case and failure modes differ completely. Be explicit which claim underlies the thesis.
- **Cycle context**: where price sits vs prior cycle highs, halving cycle for BTC, funding rates and leverage in the system as froth signals. Drawdowns of 70–85% are historical norm, not tail risk.
- **Adoption signals over price**: on-chain activity, ETF/institutional flows, developer activity, regulatory direction (MiCA in the EU) — say which, if any, actually support the thesis.
- **Position sizing**: treat as the portfolio's highest-volatility sleeve; size so a 80% drawdown is survivable and does not force selling equities. For an aggressive profile 2–10% is a common range; rebalancing bands matter more than entry precision.
- **Custody and venue risk**: exchange balances are counterparty risk (broker apps like Revolut typically do not allow withdrawal to self-custody); self-custody trades that for key-management risk. Flag which applies.
- **EUR investor specifics**: returns compound EUR/USD moves; Spanish taxation treats crypto gains as savings income like equities, but there is no two-month wash-sale rule quirk exemption — loss harvesting mechanics still apply per CNMV/AEAT guidance (verify current rules).

Never present price targets as forecasts; frame scenarios instead.
