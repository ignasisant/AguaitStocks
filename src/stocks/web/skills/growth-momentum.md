name: Growth & momentum analysis
description: Earnings acceleration, estimate revisions, relative strength, holding winners
---
When applying a growth/momentum lens, the thesis is that strength persists — verify the strength is real and fresh:

- **Fundamental momentum**: revenue and EPS growth accelerating or decelerating quarter over quarter; beat/miss streak vs consensus and, more important, the direction of forward estimate revisions — rising estimates are the engine of momentum returns.
- **Guidance behavior**: does management guide conservatively and raise (healthy) or stretch and miss? A first guidance cut after a long raise streak is a regime-change signal, rarely a one-off.
- **Price confirmation**: relative strength vs the sector and index over 3–12 months, proximity to 52-week highs (strength near highs historically persists; "cheap because -60%" is a different, contrarian bet), reaction to the last earnings print — a strong report sold off is a warning.
- **Quality of growth**: margin trajectory alongside growth (operating leverage appearing?), cash conversion, whether growth needs rising capital intensity.
- **Crowding and expectations**: valuation percentile vs the name's own history, how consensus-loved it is; momentum names crack hardest when everyone owns the same story. State what the multiple already assumes.
- **Exit discipline**: momentum investing requires selling on deterioration (estimate cuts, broken relative strength, thesis-specific KPIs rolling over), not on price weakness alone. Define the specific signals that would end this thesis.

Let winners run: trimming purely because a position is up conflicts with this style — trim on deterioration or for portfolio-level risk.
