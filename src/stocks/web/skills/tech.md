description: Technology and growth companies — SaaS metrics, moats, unit economics, valuation vs growth
---
When analysing a technology or growth company, work through:

- **Growth quality**: revenue growth rate and its trend (accelerating or decelerating), organic vs acquired, net revenue retention for subscription models. Deceleration below ~20% for a richly valued name is a thesis risk.
- **Unit economics**: gross margin level and trend (software should trend >70%; hardware/marketplaces lower), Rule of 40 (revenue growth % + FCF margin %) for SaaS, CAC payback if disclosed.
- **Moat**: network effects, switching costs, data advantage, developer ecosystem, scale economics. Name the moat explicitly and whether it is widening or narrowing; "great product" alone is not a moat.
- **Dilution**: stock-based compensation as % of revenue and share count growth. Adjust "profitability" claims for SBC — non-GAAP margins that exclude heavy SBC overstate economics.
- **Valuation vs growth**: EV/Sales or EV/FCF relative to growth rate and margins, compared against a peer set, not in isolation. State what growth the current multiple already prices in and what has to go right.
- **Key risks**: platform dependence (App Store, Google search, hyperscalers), AI disruption or tailwind, customer concentration, competition from bundled offerings.

Anchor claims in the latest reported quarter where possible and distinguish facts from your estimates.
