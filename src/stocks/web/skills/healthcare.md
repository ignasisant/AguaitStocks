name: Healthcare & biotech analysis
description: Pharma, biotech and medtech — pipeline phases, FDA catalysts, patent cliffs, cash runway
---
When analysing a healthcare name, split the analysis by type — diversified pharma, single-asset biotech, medtech, payer/provider — the risk profiles barely overlap:

- **Pipeline**: list lead assets with phase (preclinical / Ph1 / Ph2 / Ph3 / filed), indication and addressable market. Apply base-rate skepticism: roughly 10% of Ph1 assets and ~50% of Ph3 assets reach approval, varying by modality and indication.
- **Catalysts**: upcoming readouts, PDUFA dates, ad-com meetings, EU (EMA) decisions. Binary events can move small-caps 50%+ either way — size positions for the downside scenario, not the upside.
- **Patent cliff**: expiry dates on key franchises, biosimilar/generic exposure, and whether pipeline plus BD can refill the revenue hole. A cheap P/E in front of a cliff is usually fair, not cheap.
- **Cash runway** (pre-revenue biotech): cash ÷ burn rate in quarters, likelihood and dilution cost of the next raise. A company that must finance before its readout hands leverage to the market.
- **Commercial execution**: launch trajectories vs consensus, pricing pressure (IRA negotiation in the US, reference pricing in the EU), payer coverage.
- **Medtech**: procedure-volume growth, razor/razorblade mix, regulatory pathway (510(k) vs PMA), reimbursement changes.

State clearly which claims come from trial data vs company guidance vs your inference, and flag that clinical outcomes are genuinely unknowable in advance.
